Read-back of the active radio settings of the hub module (python3 lora_at.py), 14 November 2026.
The module had been power-cycled, so the band shows the factory default; the hub sets 868 MHz at every start.

Firmware:   RYLR998_REYAX_V1.2.4
Address:    0
Network ID: 3
Band:       915000000 Hz   (factory default after power-up; not stored in flash)
Parameter:  9,7,1,12  ->  SF9, 125 kHz, CR 4/5, preamble 12
Power:      20 dBm

AT+PARAMETER=12,7,1,8  ->  +ERR=4
Active after the command: 9,7,1,12  ->  SF9, 125 kHz, CR 4/5, preamble 12

AT+BAND=868000000      ->  +OK
AT+BAND?               ->  868000000

Conclusion: the firmware rejects spreading factor 12 with an 8-symbol preamble, which the code used for the
field measurements had requested, and the module keeps SF9, 125 kHz, CR 4/5, 12-symbol preamble. All field
measurements of the paper were therefore taken with that setting at 868 MHz and 20 dBm.
